DEFINING PROFESSIONAL MAHJONG

The HKPM League is dedicated to the elevation of Mahjong as a high-stakes professional sport. We maintain the highest standards of integrity, competition, and sportsmanship, bringing together the finest minds in the game. Our platform provides detailed analytics, tournament hosting, and global rankings for certified professional players. We believe in the deep complexity and tactical brilliance of Mahjong, treating every tile played as a move on a grand chessboard.
